# Maturity

One scale for every skill. Where is the technology on the path from idea to
boring.

| stage | meaning | tell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `concept` | described, not demonstrated | papers, patents, talks |
| `lab` | demonstrated under controlled conditions | prototypes, benchmarks |
| `pilot` | deployed by a few, at small scale, with support | case studies, waitlists |
| `early` | buyable; early adopters pay | pricing pages, first competitors |
| `growth` | mainstream adoption is underway | standards, regulation, M&A |
| `mature` | default choice; innovation moves to the edges | commodity pricing |
| `declining` | being replaced | sunset notices, migration guides |

`maturity` describes *now*. `horizon` on the card is the guess at when it
crosses into `growth`.
