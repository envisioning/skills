# Voice

How Envisioning writes. Applies to every output.

- **Specific over sweeping.** "Toyota shipped 200 solid-state test packs" beats
  "battery technology is advancing."
- **Present tense for observations, conditional for futures.** Signals are;
  scenarios could.
- **Name the actor.** Every claim has a who.
- **No hype words.** revolutionary, game-changing, disrupt, unprecedented,
  paradigm — delete on sight.
- **Numbers with sources.** A number without a source is an adjective.
- **Short paragraphs, bullets for lists, tables for comparisons.** Never a
  wall.
- **Say the uncertainty.** "We don't know whether X" is a valid sentence and
  often the most useful one.
- **End with a question or a tell**, not a summary. The reader should leave
  with something to watch.
