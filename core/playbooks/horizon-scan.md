# Horizon Scan

> Ported from the Signals AI playbook `horizon-scan`. The same structure runs at scale in [Signals AI](https://signals.envisioning.com).

Surfaces emerging signals of change across the macro environment so leadership can anticipate shifts that shape long-horizon strategy. Unbounded in scope, grounded in open literature, policy artifacts, scientific publications, and reputable press.

- **Audience:** Foresight, strategy, and executive leadership
- **Use when:** Tracking weak signals across the macro environment to inform long-horizon strategy, scenario work, and board narratives.

## Boundary

Identify concrete, observable developments across the macro environment with the potential to disrupt the status quo or indicate a larger shift. Stay grounded in publicly documented evidence — open literature, policy artifacts, scientific publications, datasets, and reputable press. Skip developments that lack any defensible public footprint.

## What counts as a signal here

Each item is a **specific development** — a programme, policy, dataset, demonstrated capability, or measured shift — not a generic trend headline or pure market narrative.

## Categories (pick exactly one)

| category | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Social | Social and demographic changes — values, behaviours, communities, identity, and population shifts. |
| Technological | Scientific and technological advances — new capabilities, methods, and infrastructure. |
| Economic | Economic and business developments — markets, trade, labour, capital, and industry structure. |
| Environmental | Environmental and ecological shifts — climate, biodiversity, resources, and the built environment. |
| Political | Political and regulatory changes — governance, policy, geopolitics, and institutional power. |

## Metrics (score every signal on each)

### Impact (`impact`)

How strongly the development could reshape the macro environment.

1 · Minimal · 2 · Low · 3 · Medium · 4 · High · 5 · Very High

## Sourcing

Search the web for credible primary sources that substantiate or refute this horizon signal. Prefer official publications, peer-reviewed research, government and intergovernmental data, and reporting from established outlets. Avoid blog posts, link aggregators, and AI-generated summaries.

## Merging

Merge near-duplicate horizon signals that point to the same underlying development. Prefer the title that is specific, concrete, and free of marketing language. When merging, preserve the stronger evidence hook in the summary.

## Defaults

- signals per category: 5
- sources per signal: up to 5
