# Competitive Scan

> Ported from the Signals AI playbook `competitive-scan`. The same structure runs at scale in [Signals AI](https://signals.envisioning.com).

Tracks the moves of a named set of competitors within a category — product, pricing, GTM, hiring, capital, and messaging — so leadership can see where rivals are placing bets and how their posture is shifting. Grounded in product pages, filings, job postings, public messaging, and reputable trade press.

- **Audience:** Strategy, competitive intelligence, and corporate development
- **Use when:** Tracking a defined set of competitors' behaviour within a category to inform strategy and GTM.

## Boundary

Identify concrete moves by the **competitor set and category** defined for this run. Stay **inside that boundary**; do not surface generic market commentary or non-competitor news. Skip moves that lack any defensible public footprint (no press, filing, job post, product page, or named executive comment).

## What counts as a signal here

Each item is a **specific observed move by a named competitor** — a launch, pricing change, deal, hire pattern, capital event, or public commitment — not a general theme or analyst opinion.

## Categories (pick exactly one)

| category | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Product Moves | Launches, releases, deprecations, capability changes, and platform shifts. |
| Pricing & Packaging | Price changes, new tiers, bundling, discounts, and commercial-model shifts. |
| GTM & Partnerships | Channel moves, alliances, integrations, distribution deals, and ecosystem plays. |
| Hiring & Org | Executive moves, role-pattern shifts, geographic expansion, and team-building signals from open roles. |
| Funding & M&A | Capital raises, acquisitions, divestitures, and material public-market activity. |
| Public Messaging | Positioning shifts, narrative changes, analyst-day commitments, and major customer claims. |

## Metrics (score every signal on each)

### Strategic Threat (`strategic-threat`)

How materially the move threatens our position within the category.

1 · Minimal · 2 · Low · 3 · Medium · 4 · High · 5 · Very High

### Momentum (`momentum`)

How active and accelerating the competitor's commitment to this move appears.

1 · Dormant · 2 · Tentative · 3 · Active · 4 · Accelerating · 5 · Sustained

### Confidence (`confidence`)

How well-evidenced the signal is across independent public sources.

1 · Speculative · 2 · Thin · 3 · Plausible · 4 · Strong · 5 · Confirmed

## Sourcing

Search the web for credible **primary** sources that substantiate or refute this competitive signal **for the named competitor and category**. Prefer the competitor's own filings, press releases, product pages, and job postings; named-executive interviews and analyst-day transcripts; reputable trade press. Avoid SEO blog posts, vendor-sponsored content, and AI-generated overview pages.

## Merging

Merge near-duplicate competitive signals that describe the same underlying move by the same competitor. Prefer the title that names the competitor and the move specifically and avoids marketing framing. When merging, preserve the stronger primary-source hook in the summary.

## Defaults

- signals per category: 5
- sources per signal: up to 5
