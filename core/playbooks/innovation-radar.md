# Innovation Radar

> Ported from the Signals AI playbook `innovation-radar`. The same structure runs at scale in [Signals AI](https://signals.envisioning.com).

Maps emerging practices, business models, and operating patterns that change how work gets done — distinct from raw technology, focused on the shapes of new value creation. Grounded in public case studies, company disclosures, conference talks, scholarly work, and reputable business press.

- **Audience:** Innovation, transformation, and business strategy
- **Use when:** Spotting emerging practices and models reshaping how value is created in an industry or function.

## Boundary

Identify concrete emerging **practices, models, and operating patterns** — not raw technologies — for the **scan scope** defined for this run (an industry, function, or value chain). Stay **inside that boundary**; do not broaden into generic management theory. Skip patterns without a named practitioner, case study, or primary disclosure.

## What counts as a signal here

Each item is a **specific observed practice, model, or pattern** with an identifiable practitioner — not a generic recommendation or analyst trend label.

## Categories (pick exactly one)

| category | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Business Model | New revenue, pricing, ownership, and value-capture structures. |
| Operating Model | New ways of organizing teams, accountability, decision-rights, and capability deployment. |
| Process | New methods, workflows, and ways of executing repeat activities. |
| Product | New product shapes, form factors, bundling, and category-defining offers. |
| Channel | New routes to market, distribution patterns, and customer-acquisition models. |
| Customer Experience | New service standards, interaction patterns, and end-to-end experiences. |

## Metrics (score every signal on each)

### Novelty (`novelty`)

How materially the practice or model departs from established norms in the scan scope.

1 · Incremental · 2 · Notable · 3 · Distinct · 4 · Substantial · 5 · Category-defining

### Adoption Momentum (`adoption-momentum`)

How broadly and quickly the pattern is being picked up by credible practitioners.

1 · Isolated · 2 · Early · 3 · Spreading · 4 · Accelerating · 5 · Mainstream

### Strategic Fit (`strategic-fit`)

How well the pattern fits the scan owner's strategic posture and operating reality.

1 · Poor · 2 · Weak · 3 · Plausible · 4 · Strong · 5 · Native

## Sourcing

Search the web for credible sources that substantiate or refute this innovation signal **within the scan scope**. Prefer named-practitioner case studies, company engineering and operations posts, conference talks and transcripts, peer-reviewed management research, and reputable business press. Avoid generic consulting marketing, listicle-style posts, and AI-generated overview pages.

## Merging

Merge near-duplicate innovation signals that name the same underlying practice or model. Prefer the title that is concrete, names the pattern crisply, and avoids consulting jargon. When merging, preserve the stronger named-practitioner hook in the summary.

## Defaults

- signals per category: 5
- sources per signal: up to 5
