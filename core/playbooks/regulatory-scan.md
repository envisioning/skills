# Regulatory Scan

> Ported from the Signals AI playbook `regulatory-scan`. The same structure runs at scale in [Signals AI](https://signals.envisioning.com).

Tracks rulemaking, enforcement, and standards activity for a defined jurisdiction and topic so organizations can see what is coming, what is in force, and how it is being interpreted. Grounded in primary regulator publications, statutes, court rulings, standards bodies, and reputable legal press.

- **Audience:** Legal, policy, compliance, and public affairs
- **Use when:** Following rulemaking and enforcement within a specific jurisdiction and topic area.

## Boundary

Identify concrete regulatory developments — proposed or enacted rules, enforcement actions, court rulings, industry self-regulation, and standards-body activity — for the **scan scope** defined for this run (a named jurisdiction and topic area). Stay **inside that boundary**; do not broaden into adjacent topics or jurisdictions. Skip developments without a primary regulator, statute, ruling, or filing on record.

## What counts as a signal here

Each item is a **specific regulatory artifact or action** — not a general policy theme, lobbying narrative, or media commentary.

## Categories (pick exactly one)

| category | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed Rule | Draft regulations, NPRMs, consultations, and rules open for public comment. |
| Enacted Rule | Adopted regulations, statutes, and binding instruments now in force or with a set effective date. |
| Enforcement Action | Investigations, fines, settlements, consent decrees, and other enforcement outcomes. |
| Industry Self-Regulation | Voluntary codes, industry-association rules, and self-regulatory commitments. |
| Court Ruling | Judicial decisions, opinions, and precedents that bear on the rule landscape. |
| Standards Body | Activity from standards-development organizations and technical consortia that shapes compliance practice. |

## Metrics (score every signal on each)

### Compliance Impact (`compliance-impact`)

How materially the development changes obligations or risk exposure within the scan scope.

1 · Minimal · 2 · Low · 3 · Medium · 4 · High · 5 · Very High

### Time to Effect (`time-to-effect`)

When obligations or consequences begin to bite, based on stated effective dates or enforcement posture.

1 · Immediate · 2 · Within months · 3 · 1–2 years · 4 · 2–5 years · 5 · Beyond 5 years

### Jurisdictional Breadth (`jurisdictional-breadth`)

How widely the rule, ruling, or standard applies across jurisdictions.

1 · Local · 2 · Sub-national · 3 · National · 4 · Regional bloc · 5 · Multilateral / global

## Sourcing

Search the web for credible **primary** regulatory sources that substantiate or refute this signal **within the scan's jurisdiction and topic**. Prefer official regulator publications, statutes, court opinions, agency press releases, standards-body documents, and reputable legal trade press. Avoid law-firm marketing posts that summarize other coverage, partisan commentary, and AI-generated overview pages.

## Merging

Merge near-duplicate regulatory signals that refer to the same rule, docket, ruling, or enforcement matter. Prefer the title that names the artifact specifically and avoids advocacy framing. When merging, preserve the stronger primary-source hook in the summary.

## Defaults

- signals per category: 5
- sources per signal: up to 5
