# Technology Scan

> Ported from the Signals AI playbook `technology-scan`. The same structure runs at scale in [Signals AI](https://signals.envisioning.com).

Maps publicly documented technologies within a defined scope — industry, sector, technical field, or region — so leadership can see what is real, who is building it, and how mature it is. Grounded in open literature, the product landscape, standards bodies, and regulatory context.

- **Audience:** Innovation, R&D, and technology strategy
- **Use when:** Mapping the technology landscape inside a defined industry, sector, technical field, or region.

## Boundary

Identify **public** technologies — capabilities **documented in open sources** (products, technical papers, standards, filings, reputable press, vendor engineering material) — for the **scan scope** defined for this run (a named industry, sector, technical field, region, or combination). Stay **inside that boundary**; do not broaden into a generic cross-domain panorama. Skip capabilities that lack any defensible public footprint.

## What counts as a signal here

Each item is a **concrete technology or applied technical capability** (hardware, software stack, method, or integrated system) — not a broad STEEP trend, policy headline alone, or pure market narrative.

## Categories (pick exactly one)

| category | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Hardware | Physical devices, sensors, chips, materials, robots, and other tangible technology stacks. |
| Software | Platforms, models, algorithms, protocols, and code-defined systems. |
| Applications | Domain deployments, products, workflows, and integrated uses of technology. |
| Ethics & Security | Governance, safety, privacy, societal impact, and security considerations. |

## Metrics (score every signal on each)

### Technology Readiness Level (`technology-readiness-level`)

Maturity from speculative research through established deployment.

1 · Speculative · 2 · Theoretical · 3 · Conceptual · 4 · Formative · 5 · Validated · 6 · Demonstrated · 7 · Operational · 8 · Deployed · 9 · Established

### Impact (`impact`)

How strongly the capability matters within the scan scope.

1 · Minimal · 2 · Low · 3 · Medium · 4 · High · 5 · Very High

### Investment Level (`investment-level`)

Observed or reported resource commitment and strategic priority.

1 · Minimal · 2 · Low · 3 · Medium · 4 · High · 5 · Very High

## Sourcing

Search the web for credible **public** sources that substantiate or refute this technology signal **within the scan's industry, sector, field, or region**. Prefer peer-reviewed work, standards or consortium publications, government or intergovernmental technical reports, company engineering posts with technical detail, and established technical press. Prefer primary artifacts over opinion. Avoid blogs that only summarize other articles, link farms, and AI-generated overview pages.

## Merging

Merge near-duplicate technology signals that name the same underlying capability, stack, or deployment pattern. Prefer the title that is a short noun phrase, specific, free of marketing language. When merging, preserve the stronger evidence hook in the summary.

## Defaults

- signals per category: 5
- sources per signal: up to 5
