## What

On 7 August 2026 the Commission and SpaceRISE signed the IRIS² implementation agreement: a 348-satellite main constellation (330 higher-LEO, 18 MEO) plus a separate 66-satellite higher-LEO layer for defence, security and emergency services, with first launches in 2029. The agreement claims 60% more secure governmental capacity within the EU than the prior design.

## So what

- European satellite manufacturers: the largest single European constellation order in history is now contracted, not planned.
- Launch providers: 414 satellites need rides from 2029 — a demand anchor for Ariane 6 and the new launchers.
- Member states: a sovereign, accredited connectivity layer changes procurement defaults for government communications.

## Watch for

- First satellite delivery dates from the LEO primes (Aerospacelab, Airbus) — slips here move everything.
- Whether the 66-satellite defence layer is funded from the same budget or triggers a separate defence line.
