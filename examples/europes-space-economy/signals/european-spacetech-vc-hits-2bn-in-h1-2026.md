## What

European space ventures raised $2.0 billion in the first half of 2026, against $1.3 billion for all of 2025, led by Isar Aerospace's €270 million Series D and PLD Space's €180 million Series C with Mitsubishi Electric as lead investor and customer. Launch companies took the bulk of it.

## So what

- Founders outside launch: the capital is concentrated; EO, in-orbit services and components compete for the remainder.
- Strategic investors from Japan and elsewhere: European launch is now a place non-European industrials buy into.
- Public funders (ESA, EIB): the argument that Europe lacks private space capital is weaker this year than last.

## Watch for

- H2 2026 total — whether $4bn lands or H1 was two rounds.
- The first European space company to raise at a down round; the sector has not yet had one at scale.
