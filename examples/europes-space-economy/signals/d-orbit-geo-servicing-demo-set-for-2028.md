## What

D-Orbit holds a €119.6 million ESA Space Safety contract, signed in October 2024 with Italian Space Agency support, to build and fly RISE — a vehicle that docks with a geostationary satellite, moves it and releases it — with launch expected in 2028, after which D-Orbit sells life extension commercially.

## So what

- GEO operators (SES, Eutelsat): a European alternative to Northrop's MEV for extending ageing fleets.
- ESA: it is buying a service demonstration from an Italian new-space company, not building a mission.
- Italy: the country's space-industrial policy is visibly bet on in-orbit services.

## Watch for

- Critical design review and launch-contract announcements in 2026–27; silence would mean slip.
- First named commercial life-extension customer.
