## Decision

Workshop participants pick the signals, tensions and opportunities that could reshape Europe's space economy over 10–20 years, then build on them in the participatory session. The scan is the shared starting corpus; the decision is which of it they carry forward.

## Boundary

Europe's space economy: European launch, in-orbit servicing, earth observation, connectivity (IRIS², direct-to-device), navigation (Galileo), manufacturing and supply chain, financing, and the regulatory frame (EU Space Act, national laws, ESA governance). Activity by European actors anywhere, and by non-European actors where it changes the European position. Horizon 10 years, with a 20-year edge.

## Out of scope

- US and Chinese domestic programmes, except as they act on Europe
- Deep-space science without an industrial or commercial angle
- Space tourism

## Plan

1. `horizon-scan` — STEEP, up to 5 per category, then
2. `ground` every card, `merge` duplicates,
3. `frames` (tensions) for the workshop's "structural tensions" question,
4. `report` as a `readout` for the room.
